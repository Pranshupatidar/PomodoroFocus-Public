# Pomodoro Timer: Focus Bar

Support and issue tracker for **Pomodoro Timer: Focus Bar**, a native macOS focus timer.

The timer lives in a slim floating bar that stays on top of every window, follows you
across Spaces, and keeps floating over full-screen apps. It breaks your day into focused
work sessions and deliberate breaks, then tracks every minute so you can watch your
consistency build.

## Getting help

Open the [issue page](https://github.com/Pranshupatidar/PomodoroFocus-Public/issues/1)
and add a comment. Please include:

- Your macOS version
- The app version
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- Steps to reproduce, if you know them

For anything you would rather not post publicly, email patidar.pranshu21@gmail.com.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing. No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync, no analytics, no
tracking, no telemetry. Every session you log is stored locally on your Mac and never
leaves it. The app makes no network connections at all.

## Requirements

macOS 26 or later.
